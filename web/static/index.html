    Exchange
              approval-gated
    
          
    Market reads are keyless. Orders exist only as plans: this panel is where you approve or reject them — the model's execute step receives a plan id and can never approve its own plan. Withdrawals and on-chain transfers do not exist in this product at all.
    
          
      
            
    • The product is permanently free, but exchange fees and on-chain gas are not zero: you settle them yourself with the exchange or the chain.
      • 
            
    • Nothing on this panel is investment advice.
      • 
            
    • Trading risk is borne by you: you are responsible for the outcome of any trade you make.
      • 
            
    • Credentials stay on this machine (secrets.env) and are never echoed into chats, logs or documents.

      AGENT BACKUP
      
          
      a scheduled prompt runs the backup_run tool: the console exports over the local loopback, encrypts with the AIDOG_CKX_PASSWORD slot through the vendored CKX CLI, verifies the container and proves a round-trip digest. The passphrase reaches only the child process — never a command line, never this page.

Local encrypt never calls their API, so this meter is a deliberately conservative proxy; their billing record is authoritative. RESET clears only our count.
